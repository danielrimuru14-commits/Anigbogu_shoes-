Shoe Business Plan

A simple business plan for Anigbogu Chimeire's footwear business.

1. Business Idea

Sell fashionable and durable shoes for students, young people and adults. Products can include sneakers, casual shoes, school shoes and slippers.

2. Target Market

Students, young adults, workers and families looking for attractive footwear at reasonable prices.

3. Products

Quality sneakers, casual shoes, school shoes, slides and other popular footwear styles. Stock can be expanded according to customer demand.

4. Marketing

Promote through WhatsApp Status, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, referrals and attractive product photographs.

5. Sales Strategy

Offer clear prices, good customer service, product variety and occasional promotions. Take orders online and arrange convenient delivery.

6. Business Goal

Build a trusted footwear brand, increase repeat customers and gradually expand from small sales into a larger shoe business.
